FTC Discord CAD Competition · 2026

Chain Reaction Robot

Onshape1-Week SprintMechanism DesignPackaging

A competition-ready robot concept designed entirely in Onshape in one week for the 2026 FTC Discord CAD Competition. The project challenged me to turn a game strategy into a cohesive mechanical design while working against a fixed deadline.

My Role

Owning the one-week design sprint

I was responsible for developing the complete robot concept in Onshape, from early game strategy through mechanism design and final packaging. Working independently against a one-week deadline required me to prioritize high-impact features, manage my time carefully, and deliver a finished submission on schedule.

Robot capabilities

Key Features

01

12+ Ball Capacity

High-capacity onboard storage supports longer scoring cycles and reduces time spent reacquiring game pieces.

02

Full-Field Rapid Fire

A high-output shooting system is designed for rapid scoring from anywhere on the field.

03

Double X-Contact Turret

A dual X-contact turret architecture maximizes ball throughput for fast, continuous scoring.

04

Horizontal-Motor Drivetrain

Horizontally packaged drivetrain motors preserve valuable internal space for mechanisms and ball storage.
