FTC Into the Deep · Concept Design · 2026

ITD Concept Robot

A custom CAD robot developed around the scoring demands of the FTC Into the Deep game. The concept combines compact packaging with versatile mechanisms for collecting, transferring, and scoring both samples and specimens.

Onshape CADRoboticsDesign

Design approach

A versatile concept for every scoring cycle

I designed the robot as a complete system rather than a collection of separate mechanisms. The intake, transfer, slides, and articulated arms work together to support fast sample cycles while retaining the range of motion needed for specimen scoring.

Robot capabilities

Key Features

01

Linear Slide Extension

A compact linear-slide system provides the reach needed to move game elements between collection, transfer, and scoring positions.

02

Co-axial Virtual Four-Bar Intake

Allows the claw to remain parallel to the ground while collecting game elements, then rotate for transfer.

03

Multi-DOF Scoring Arms

Articulated outtake arm with multiple degrees of freedom provides flexible positioning for sample transfer, sample deposit, specimen pickup, and specimen scoring.
